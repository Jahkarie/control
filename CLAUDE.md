# On D' Road: backend (`control`)

On D' Road is an invite-only, 18+ Antigua Carnival band. Guests join through an invite chain, reserve a package, pay, and get a live QR entry pass. It also runs a public ticket site (tickets.ondroad.xyz: entry tickets and VIP tables with bottle service, no invites) for its other events. This repo is the API, the admin pages and the door scanner for both. The guest sites are a separate repo, `controlfrontend` (`index.html`, and the ticket site in `tickets/index.html`).

## Stack

- Node (ES modules) + Express 4, Postgres on Aiven (`pg`), email via Resend.
- Hosted on Render at `https://control-1-0baa.onrender.com`.
- No build step, no tests. Run with `npm start`.

## Files

- `server.js`: config, auth, schema setup, the sign-up profile validators (`cleanName`, `cleanBirthDate`, `cleanInstagram`, `profileInput`), guest/invite/profile/package/order routes, approval and the rest of the admin API, and the Command Center admin page at `/admin` (HTML inlined as `ADMIN_PAGE`).
- `payments.js`: payment settings (`payment_instructions`, `pay_days`), the pay-by deadline, a job every 10 minutes that expires unpaid orders and sends one reminder, `/api/my-order`, and the `/admin/payments` page. It is registered before the routes in `server.js`, so its `/api/my-order` wins and the one in `server.js` is an unused fallback.
- `events.js`: events, one current at a time: `CUR` (the current event's id as a fixed SQL fragment, used in every scoped query), `INVITES_LEFT_SQL` (a guest's allowance for the current event), `currentEvent()`, `createDefaultEvent()`, `migrateEvents()` (run by `ensureSchema()`), `/api/admin/events` (list with totals and the switch warning counts), `/api/admin/events/current` (rename, invites per guest), `/api/admin/events/start` (start a new event), `/api/admin/events/:id/orders.csv`, and the `/admin/events` page ("Events").
- `promoter.js`: promoters: their private page `/promoter/<token>` (an HTML string, phone-first), its API `/api/promoter/me|invites|invites/:id/revoke` (token in the `x-promoter-token` header), and the admin routes `/api/admin/promoters`, `/api/admin/promoters/:id` and `/api/admin/promoters/:id/reset-link`. Registered in `server.js` after the invite helpers it shares (`inviteBlocked`, `hashToken`, `inviteLink`).
- `event.js`: the current event's details approved guests see in their account (`/api/event`: name, when, info, paid-only info and location, poster), emails to groups of guests through Resend's batch API, and the `/admin/event` page ("Event & messages").
- `site.js`: contact details, terms, FAQ and sponsor logos (`/api/site`, public; while the site is locked it answers only `{ locked: true }`), the `/admin/site` page ("Site, contact, terms & FAQ", with the Site lock section at the top), and `DEFAULT_TERMS` / `DEFAULT_FAQ`, the drafts guests see until the organizers save their own.
- `lock.js`: the site lock, as a factory: `createLock({ keys, tokenPrefix, applies, adminPath, what })` gives the `guard` middleware (registered right after CORS, before every route), `check(req)` (used by `/api/site`), `lockedNow()` (locked for everyone, preview or not; used by promoter invites), `forget()` (clears its few-second settings cache) and `register()` for `<adminPath>` and `<adminPath>/preview-reset`. The main lock uses the defaults (`site_lock`, `/api/admin/site-lock`); tickets.js makes the ticket site's own.
- `pricing.js`: price tiers and promo codes: `loadTiers`/`currentTier`/`priceOf` (what a package costs now), `checkPromo`/`discountFor`/`promoLabel`/`adminCodeLabel`, `/api/promo/check`, `/api/orders/promo`, the tier and code admin routes, and the `/admin/pricing` page ("Prices, codes & promoters", with the Promoters section, which uses `promoter.js`'s admin routes).
- `pickup.js`: the size lock (`getSizeLock`, `isSizeLocked`), admin size changes, marking packages collected, and the `/admin/pickup` page ("Sizes & pickup").
- `reset.js`: the `/admin/reset` page ("Reset") and `/api/admin/reset`, which wipes guest data (the ticket site's orders and tickets too), or everything, to start over.
- `paypal.js`: paying online with PayPal: `/api/paypal/checkout|create-order|capture`, refunds through PayPal, and `syncDue()`, which the payments job runs before cancelling unpaid orders. Its plumbing is exported for tickets.js: `paypalApi()`, `paypalIssue()`, `captureOf()`, `orderPath()`, `refundCapture()`, `paypalEnabledFor()`, `usdCents()`/`usd()`.
- `door.js`: staff scanner at `/door` (works offline through a service worker) with a work picker (On D' Road, or a ticket event on today), an Entry and a Pickup mode, Look up (phone dead), `/api/door/list|tickets|checkin|sync`, and the door log at `/api/admin/checkins`.
- `tickets.js`: the public ticket site's backend: `ensureTicketSchema()` (run by `ensureSchema()`), its own lock (`createLock()` with the `tickets_*` settings), the guest API under `/api/tickets/*`, checkout and the capacity holds, PayPal (in full, a table's deposit, its balance), the signed order and ticket pages, transfers, "find my tickets", its emails, `sweep()` (the job, run by the payments job every 10 minutes), the admin API under `/api/admin/tickets/*`, `/api/admin/tickets-lock`, `/api/admin/ticket-checkins`, the bar list and door list, `DEFAULT_TICKET_TERMS`, and `createTicketDoor()` (the ticket side of the door). `registerTickets()` is called before `express.json` (its routes bring their own parsers).
- `tickets-admin.js`: the `/admin/tickets` page ("Tickets & tables": Events, Menus, Ticket site lock, Terms) and the printable bar list, as HTML strings.
- `liveqr.js`: the live QR: `qrSecret()`, `qrCode()`, `codeOk()` (this 30-second step ± 2) and `BROWSER_CODE` (the same in WebCrypto, pasted into the door page).
- `brand.js`: the CASHCXDE ENT. watermark: `WM_LITE` / `WM_INK` (SVG data-URI tiles for the pages' `--wm`), `WM_PNG` (the email tile, served at `/brand/wm.png`) and `COPYRIGHT`.
- `emails.js`: `renderEmail()` builds every email (HTML + plain text; `site: 'tickets'`, `brandTag`, `ticketList`, `callouts` and `links` for the ticket emails). The header carries the watermark band (`/brand/wm.png` as a background, over a solid colour, when `PUBLIC_URL` is known); the footer lists the contact details set through `setContact()` (called by `site.js` on boot and after each save) and `© CASHCXDE ENT.`.

## How it works

- **Site lock:** the whole guest site shows only the trailer until the owner opens it. Settings `site_lock` (`'on'` or `'off'`; a **missing key means locked**, so a fresh database, the first deploy and "Reset everything" start locked), `site_unlock_at` (ISO time or `''`) and `preview_version` (a counter). Locked = `site_lock !== 'off'` and not (`site_unlock_at` set and now ≥ it); the time is checked on every request (the settings are cached for 5 seconds; saves and a full reset clear the cache), so no job is needed, and a time that can't be read never opens it. While locked, `lock.guard` answers every guest route (anything under `/api/` except `/api/admin/*`, `/api/door/*`, `/api/promoter/*`, `/api/pass/*`, `/api/tickets/*` (the ticket site has its own lock, below) and `/api/site`, matched ignoring case, unknown paths included; `OPEN_ROUTES` in lock.js) with `423 { error: 'Not open yet.', code: 'SITE_LOCKED' }`, so logins, invites, sign-ups, orders, PayPal and the rest are off; admin pages, the door scanner, signed pass images, the promoters' pages (`/promoter/<token>` isn't under `/api/`, so the guard never sees it, and their API is open) and the background jobs keep working. A promoter sending an invite while locked gets `423 SITE_LOCKED` from `promoter.js` itself ("The site is locked, so nobody could accept an invite yet."), since nobody could open the link. `/api/site` answers only `{ locked: true }` (no contact, terms, FAQ, sponsors or unlock time; `Cache-Control: no-store`). The owner's way in is the preview link `${FRONTEND_URL or https://ondroad.xyz}/?preview=<token>`, token = HMAC(`TOKEN_SECRET`, `'preview:' + preview_version`), base64url; the guest site sends it back as an `x-preview` header (compared in constant time), and a valid one gets through exactly as if the site were open (`/api/site` then adds `preview: true` to its usual body with `locked: false`). Resetting the link bumps `preview_version`, which kills the old one. Admin: `GET /api/admin/site-lock` → `{ site_lock, site_unlock_at, locked (effective), preview_url }`; `POST /api/admin/site-lock { site_lock: 'on'|'off', site_unlock_at: ISO|'' }` (either; the time must be a real date in the future, and a time without a zone is Antigua time; switching off clears the time, and switching on drops a time that has already passed); `POST /api/admin/site-lock/preview-reset`. All through `requireAdmin` (CSRF guard included). `/admin/site` has a Locked / Open switch, "Unlock automatically at" (a datetime-local in Antigua time, UTC-4, with Set time and Clear), the state line ("Locked. Opens on Sat 1 Nov, 8:00 PM (Antigua time)." / "Open." / "Opened automatically on …") and Copy / Reset preview link (both switch directions and the reset ask first); `/admin` shows a slim "Site is locked · … Manage" line while locked.
- **Events:** the `events` table holds every event; exactly one is `status = 'current'` (a partial unique index, `events_one_current`), the rest `'past'`. The roster is shared by all of them: guests, approvals and profiles carry over (approval is global: approved once, approved for every event; pending and declined requests stay as they are). Everything guests buy and do belongs to one event through `event_id` on `packages`, `orders` (and so their check-ins and pickups), `promo_codes`, `broadcasts` and `invites`, and everything guest-facing is the current event's: `/api/packages`, `/api/orders` (one active order per guest **per event**), `/api/my-order`, `/api/event` (which also returns `name`, the title of the guest's event page), code checks, PayPal, the payments job, the refund flow, the door, pickup, the size totals, group emails to paid/reserved guests, `has_paid` (so pay-first, the RSVP rule and the profile lock are per event) and the admin Orders/Door tabs (`?event=<id>` shows another event's, `?event=all` every event's). An event also holds its details (`event_when`, `event_info`, `event_paid_info`, `event_location`, `event_poster`), `invites_per_guest`, `started_at`/`ended_at` and, once past, `rsvp_confirmed` (how many had confirmed). Queries scope with `CUR` from events.js; `event_id` also defaults to `current_event_id()` (a SQL function), so a row written without one (an older backend during a deploy, SQL by hand) lands in the current event. `/admin/events` ("Events", first in the overview's nav) shows the current event (rename, invites per guest, totals: reserved, paid, money received per currency, check-ins), past events (read-only summaries, an orders CSV with the UTF-8 BOM, and "View orders", which opens `/admin?event=<id>#orders`: the overview's Orders tab on that event, read-only), and "Start a new event" (name, invites per guest, "copy packages"). Starting one shows the counts first (unpaid reservations, which are cancelled with no email; open refund requests, which **block** it; PayPal payments in flight, which also block it; paid guests, whose passes stay as history; confirmed attendance; pending invites, which stay open; packages to copy) and needs the new name typed again (case and extra spaces ignored; `CONFIRM_MISMATCH` otherwise). `POST /api/admin/events/start { name, invites_per_guest, copy_packages, confirm }` then does it all in one transaction: lock the current event row (reservations, invites, codes and PayPal checkouts hold it `FOR SHARE`, so none lands in the old event halfway), refuse with `409 REFUNDS_PENDING`/`PAYPAL_BUSY`, cancel the unpaid reservations, old → past (`ended_at`, `rsvp_confirmed`), set every `guests.rsvp_status` back to `PENDING`, new → current (`started_at`), and copy the packages (hidden, `active = false`; with includes, sizes, pay note, capacity, sort order and tiers, a tier's end date only when still to come). Codes aren't copied, the event details start empty, promoters' invites for it start at 0, and payment settings, terms, FAQ, contact and the size lock are site-wide and carry over. Past events are read-only: their packages, codes, orders (mark paid, cancel, refund, size, pickup) can't change. A past event's pass at the door scans as "Wrong event" (below); its emailed pass image still loads (history).
- **Promoters:** `promoters` (`name`, `note`, `active`, `token_version`) and `promoter_event` (`promoter_id`, `event_id`, `invites`: their allowance for that event). A promoter has codes (`promo_codes.promoter_id`; any kind, `TRACK` included) and invites, and a private page at `/promoter/<token>`: token = `<id>.<HMAC(TOKEN_SECRET, 'promoter:' + id + ':' + token_version)>` (base64url), compared in constant time; a wrong token gets a 404 "Link not working" page and `401 LINK_INVALID` from the API. The page (`no-store`, `no-referrer`, `noindex`) carries no data: its script reads the token from the address and calls `/api/promoter/me` with it in `x-promoter-token`, which returns, for the current event, their codes with uses, orders reserved/paid/cancelled through their codes, money received (paid only, per currency), invites (`sent`, `pending`, `accepted` = joined, `approved`, `paid`, `allowance`, `left`), their pending invites (id, the email they typed, when; so they can revoke) and a small past-events summary. **No guest names, emails or Instagram handles** otherwise: only counts. `POST /api/promoter/invites { email, note }` sends an invite for the current event from their allowance (`403 NO_INVITES` when used up): like a guest's (single-use, tied to the email, only the hash stored, the same eligibility through `inviteBlocked()`), stored with `inviter_email = 'PROMOTER'` and `promoter_id`, emailed as "<Name> invited you", refused while the site is locked, and limited to 20 an hour per promoter and 60 an hour per IP. `POST /api/promoter/invites/:id/revoke` revokes one of theirs; the admin's revoke works too; either gives the invite back. The invited person signs up as `PENDING` like a guest-invited one; `/api/invite/:token` answers `{ inviter: <promoter name>, inviter_kind: 'PROMOTER', note }`, and the admin's "New request" email, the Requests tab and the Invites tab say "Invited by promoter <name>" / "Promoter <name>". Admin (the Promoters section of `/admin/pricing`): create and edit (name, note, active, invites for the current event), live stats per promoter (the same numbers, plus who: the guests through their codes and those who joined, by name), Copy link and Reset link (asks first; bumps `token_version`, so the old link dies). Turning a promoter off kills their link and their codes stop applying (`checkPromo`); orders already made stay.
- **Invites:** a guest's allowance for the current event is `events.invites_per_guest` (2 by default) + their bonus for it (`guest_event.invite_bonus`, set by the admin's "Set invites", which stores whatever bonus makes "invites left" the number typed; `/api/admin/reset-invites` sets it back to 0) − the invites they sent for this event that aren't revoked (`INVITES_LEFT_SQL`). `/api/user-status` and `/api/admin/guests` return that as `invites_left` (the admin list adds `invite_bonus` and `invites_sent`), so the guest site works unchanged. `guests.invites_left` is the legacy counter: the migration turned it into each guest's bonus (bonus = `invites_left` + their non-revoked invites − 2, so every allowance stayed the same), and nothing reads or writes it any more (new rows still get the column's default). Guests can only send invites once they have a `PAID` order for the current event (a free pass counts); before that `/api/send-invite` answers `403` with `code: 'PAY_FIRST'` (and `NOT_APPROVED` to anyone who isn't approved). Sending one creates a single-use link (`?invite=<token>`) tied to the recipient's email and the current event; only the token's hash is stored. Nobody on the roster (pending and declined requests included) or with a pending invite can be invited again; someone removed from the roster can, unless they still have an order for the current event that isn't cancelled (someone removed back when a paid guest could be; the admin uses "Add to roster" for them). Admin invites (`inviter_email = 'CONTROL'`) and promoter invites (`'PROMOTER'`) don't use any guest's allowance. Revoking a pending invite gives it back. Pending invites stay open when a new event starts (they count against the event they were sent in), and the Invites tab lists them with "From <event>".
- **Sign-up profile:** accepting an invite (`/api/invite/accept`) needs `full_name` (as on their ID), `birth_date` (`'YYYY-MM-DD'`) and `instagram`, stored on `guests`. The date of birth is the 18+ check (age 18 to 100 on today's date in Antigua; turning 18 today counts), so there's no separate 18+ box (the guest site still sends `adult: true`, which is ignored). Names are tidied first (NFKC, so styled letters copied from a bio become plain ones; curly apostrophes and dashes made plain, invisible characters including the Hangul fillers dropped, spaces collapsed) and must be 2-80 letters, accents and `' . , -`, starting with a letter. Instagram accepts `@name` or an instagram.com profile link (a post, reel or story link is refused) and is stored lowercase without the `@`. Bad input gets `400` with `code` `PROFILE_REQUIRED` (missing, or a date that isn't real), `NAME_INVALID`, `UNDER_18` or `INSTAGRAM_INVALID`, plus `field`; it's checked before the invite is touched, so a mistake never uses it up. A page cached from before the form sends no fields and gets "Reload the page and fill in your details." If the admin had already added the guest, only their missing fields are filled in. `POST /api/profile` (`20/hour` per account) lets guests fill in or change their details: name and date of birth are locked once they have a `PAID` order for the current event (`403`, `code: 'PROFILE_LOCKED'`) unless empty; Instagram can always change. Admin-added and older guests fill it in once before reserving.
- **Approval:** a guest who joins through a guest's invite starts as `guests.approval = 'PENDING'` (with `applied_at`); the admin's own invites and admin-added guests are `APPROVED`, as is every guest from before approvals. Each new request emails `ADMIN_EMAIL` ("New request: <Name>", with age, Instagram, email and who invited them, and a "Review requests" button to `/admin#requests` when `PUBLIC_URL`/`RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL` is set) and shows a banner on `/admin`. The Requests tab approves (`/api/admin/guests/approve`, from `PENDING` or `DECLINED`; emails "You're approved" with a link to the site) or declines (`/api/admin/guests/decline`, only from `PENDING`; no email, the invite stays used and the inviter doesn't get it back). Both set `decided_at`. Until approved, `/api/orders` answers `403` `code: 'NOT_APPROVED'` (so do `/api/send-invite`, `/api/update-rsvp`, `/api/orders/promo` and `/api/paypal/create-order`, through `requireApproved` or their own check), `/api/promo/check` refuses, and `/api/event` returns empty details. `/api/user-status` returns the guest row (`birth_date` as `'YYYY-MM-DD'`, since `server.js` makes pg return DATE columns as text) plus `has_paid` (a `PAID` order for the current event), `invited_by` (inviter email, `'CONTROL'`, `'PROMOTER'` or null), `invited_by_name` (the inviting guest's name, or the promoter's) and `invites_left` (for the current event); `/api/login/verify` and `/api/invite/accept` return `user.approval`. The admin can edit anyone's profile at any time (`/api/admin/guests/profile`; `''` clears a field).
- **Attendance (RSVP):** `guests.rsvp_status` (`PENDING` or `CONFIRMED`, shown as "Attendance" in admin) is the current event's: starting a new event sets everyone back to `PENDING` in the same transaction and keeps the old event's count in `events.rsvp_confirmed` (one column and one reset rather than a per-event table, since only the event that's on needs it). `/api/update-rsvp` only confirms once the guest has a `PAID` order for the current event (`403`, `code: 'PAY_FIRST'`), and it's locked once confirmed: asking for `PENDING` then gets `403` `code: 'RSVP_LOCKED'`. To pull out, the guest requests a refund. Approving it sets attendance back to `PENDING`. It doesn't affect entry: only a paid pass gets someone in.
- **Login:** passwordless. `/api/login` emails a one-time link (`?login=<code>`, 15 minutes). `/api/login/verify` exchanges it for an HMAC-signed token valid for 7 days. The frontend sends `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
- **Admin auth:** `ADMIN_KEY` via the `x-admin-key` header or HTTP Basic (password = key). Door staff use `DOOR_KEY` (the admin key also works) via `x-door-key`. Because the browser resends a saved Basic login to any request, `requireAdmin` only accepts changes (anything but GET/HEAD) made with Basic auth when they are same-origin JSON requests, as the admin pages send; a form or fetch from another site gets `403` (CSRF guard).
- **Orders:** one active order per guest per event (`orders.event_id`; a past event's pass doesn't count). Each order stores what it costs when it's reserved: `tier_id`/`tier_name`, `list_cents` (before any code), `discount_cents`, `amount_cents`, `currency` and `promo_code`. Always read money from the order (`COALESCE(o.amount_cents, p.price_cents)`, `COALESCE(o.currency, p.currency)`), never from the package, so price changes don't touch existing orders. `RESERVED` → `PAID` (the guest pays with PayPal, or the admin marks a cash payment paid; `orders.paid_via` is `PAYPAL` or `MANUAL`) or `CANCELLED` (guest cancels, admin cancels, deadline expires, or a refund is approved). Paid guests can request a refund and withdraw the request; the pass keeps working until the admin approves. References look like `ODR-1A2B3C4D`.
- **Packages:** price in cents, currency `XCD` or `USD`, optional capacity. `active = false` hides a package ("Visible/Hidden" in admin). `requires_compliance` means the costume rules apply. `pay_note` is the package's own cash payment details, shown under the general "How to pay" while an order is reserved and in its emails; `/api/packages` returns it too (empty string when not set), so the guest site can show "Paying cash?" on the package card.
- **Price tiers:** `package_tiers` (name, price, optional `ends_at`, optional `quantity`, `sort_order`). Guests pay the first tier that hasn't ended or sold out (sold = the tier's non-cancelled orders, so an expired reservation frees its spot). When every tier has ended or sold out, the package's sales are closed (`/api/orders` answers `SOLD_OUT`). A package with no tiers costs its own price. `/api/packages` returns the current `price_cents`, `tier` (`{ id, name, ends_at, left }`), `next_tier` and `sales_closed`. The guest site sends the `tier_id` and `price_cents` it showed; if either changed meanwhile (a new tier, or an edited price), `/api/orders` answers `409` with `code: 'PRICE_CHANGED'`.
- **Promo codes:** `promo_codes` (uppercase `code`, `kind` `PERCENT`/`AMOUNT`/`FREE`/`TRACK`, `value`, optional `package_id`, `max_uses`, `expires_at`, `active`, `event_id`, optional `promoter_id`). Only the current event's codes work, codes of a promoter who's turned off don't, and codes aren't copied to a new event; code names are unique across events (`409 ... (from a past event)` when reused). `TRACK` takes nothing off (value 0, full price): it only records the promoter. Guests see it as "Code applied" (`promoLabel`; never "off" or a discount), the admin as "Tracking only (full price)" (`adminCodeLabel`), and `/api/my-order` returns `promo_kind` so the guest site words it right. The code form on `/admin/pricing` has a Promoter select and the Tracking only kind. Uses = orders with that code that aren't cancelled, so a cancelled order gives its use back. The code row is locked while an order uses it, so two guests can't both take the last use. Codes apply at reservation (`promo_code`) or to a reserved order (`/api/orders/promo`, refused once a PayPal checkout has started). A code that makes the total 0 confirms the order straight away with `paid_via = 'COMP'` (a free pass) and sends the pass email. Bad codes get `code: 'PROMO_INVALID'`. Wrong codes are counted (in memory, for a day) per account (20) and per IP (100) across `/api/promo/check`, `/api/orders/promo` and `/api/orders`; past that, any code is refused with `429` (`code: 'PROMO_INVALID'`) so codes can't be guessed. `/api/promo/check` refuses accounts no longer on the roster.
- **Reserving:** `/api/orders` refuses, in this order, guests who aren't approved (`403`, `NOT_APPROVED`) and guests with any profile field missing (`400`, `PROFILE_REQUIRED`, with "Reload the page to add them." for a page cached from before the form; the profile is only ever saved through `/api/profile`). `guests.age_confirmed_at` records when the guest gave a date of birth (or, before that, ticked the old 18+ box).
- **Event details:** stored on the current event's row (`events.event_when`, `event_info` and `event_poster` for every approved guest, `event_paid_info` and `event_location` only returned once the guest's order for this event is `PAID`, as `paid_info` and `location`); `/api/event` adds the event's `name`. They used to be `settings` keys of the same names: the migration copied them to the first event and left the keys (unused) for an older backend. `event_poster` is the event page's cover: an `https:` link or a path in the guest site repo like `art/poster.jpg`, checked like the sponsor logos (`poster`; empty means the site's own art). Guests who aren't approved get empty details. `/api/admin/event` saves only the fields it's sent. The location is only ever shown in a paid guest's account, never in emails; the organizers reveal it by group email when they choose.
- **Group emails:** to the current event's paid guests or reserved-but-unpaid guests, or everyone approved (the roster, whatever the event; pending and declined requests get no group emails), sent in batches of 100 with a short pause between batches. Each send is logged in `broadcasts` with the current event; the page shows the current event's (`?event=all` every event's). Test sends aren't logged.
- **Contact details:** `settings` keys `contact_whatsapp` (digits with country code; Antigua numbers can be typed without it), `contact_instagram` (username) and `contact_email`. Shown in the guest site's footer and at the bottom of every email.
- **Terms:** `settings.terms` (falls back to `DEFAULT_TERMS`) and `terms_updated_at`. In the text, `# ` starts a heading and `- ` a bullet point. `/api/orders` requires `terms: true` (`code: 'TERMS_REQUIRED'`) and stores `orders.terms_accepted_at`.
- **Sizes:** `packages.sizes` is a comma-separated list; empty means no size is needed. When it's set, `/api/orders` requires one of them (`code: 'SIZE_REQUIRED'`) and stores it in `orders.size`. Guests change it with `/api/orders/size` until the organizers lock sizes (`settings.size_lock`: `open`, `paid` = locked once paid, or `all`; refused with `code: 'SIZE_LOCKED'`) or their package has been collected (also `SIZE_LOCKED`; `/api/my-order` then says `size_locked: true`). The admin can always change a size (Orders tab or `/api/admin/orders/:id/size`). The admin packages list shows how many active orders picked each size. (`guests.shirt_size` and `/api/update-shirt` are old and unused.)
- **PayPal:** off until `PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID` and `PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET` are set. In sandbox mode (the default) only the emails in `PAYPAL_TESTERS` see it; `PAYPAL_ENV=live` shows it to everyone. PayPal doesn't take XCD, so XCD prices are charged in USD at 2.70 (`usdCents()`), rounded up to the cent; fees are absorbed. `create-order` makes the PayPal order (invoice id = our reference, so PayPal blocks a second completed payment) and stores `paypal_order_id`, `paypal_amount` and `paypal_status`. `capture` locks the order row, captures, checks PayPal took exactly `paypal_amount`, then marks it paid and sends the usual confirmation. A payment that completed without reaching us (page closed, lost response) is found by `settle()` when the guest taps PayPal again, or by `syncDue()`. The deadline job skips orders with a checkout started in the last 30 minutes or a payment PayPal is still holding (`paypal_status = 'PENDING'`). Approving a refund on a PayPal order refunds it through PayPal first; if PayPal refuses, nothing changes. No webhooks are used.
- **Live pass (anti-screenshot):** every paid pass and ticket shows a live QR that changes every 30 seconds (`liveqr.js`). Each has a secret, `HMAC-SHA256(TOKEN_SECRET, 'qr:' + ref + ':' + version)` (base64url; `orders.qr_version` and `tickets.version`, 1 at first, a ticket's bumps on transfer); the code is `base32(HMAC-SHA256(secret bytes, String(floor(unix seconds / 30))))`, first 8 characters (A-Z, 2-7). A pass's QR is `ONDROAD:<reference>:<code>`, a ticket's `ONDROAD-T:<ref>:<code>`. The guest's page works the code out itself with WebCrypto from the secret: `/api/my-order` adds `qr_secret` while the order is `PAID`, and the ticket site's signed order and ticket pages carry each valid ticket's `secret`; both pages keep it on the phone so the pass shows offline, and drop it once refunded, cancelled or transferred. The door accepts the step it's in ± 2 (about a minute and a half), at the scan time or now; anything else is `STALE` ("Old screenshot: ask for the live pass/ticket"). Door phones get the secrets in their list (behind `DOOR_KEY`) so they check codes offline too. The old still pass (`ONDROAD:<reference>:<email>`, and older ones) is refused as `STALE` unless `settings.door_allow_static = '1'` (no admin switch: set it by hand only if old screenshots must work again; off by default since no paid passes were out when the live QR came in). Emails carry no QR any more: the confirmation (`sendPaidEmail`, used by manual, PayPal and free-pass orders) and the ticket emails have an "Open your pass" / "Open your ticket" button and "Screenshots won't work at the door; open your pass/ticket on your phone." `/api/pass/<ref>.png` and `/api/tickets/qr/<ref>.png` are retired (`410`, plain text).
- **Watermark ("ON D' ROAD + CASHCXDE ENT."):** `brand.js` holds the tile (the sliced D' and the name, rotated about −20°, chalk at 5% for dark surfaces, ink at 6.5% for concrete). Every admin page and the door page put it in `:root` as `--wm` and show it in the header band; the door's result screens carry it too (no "LIVE" text there). Emails get it as a header band image (`/brand/wm.png`, `WM_PNG`, over a solid colour). The guest sites' own copies and the live watermark (name + Antigua clock + LIVE, drifting, with a sheen) are described in controlfrontend's CLAUDE.md. It deters casual copying and makes screenshots easy to tell from a live phone; it can't stop someone saving the original images or video.
- **Door:** the scanner reads the live QR (above). `/api/door/list` holds only the current event's passes (each paid one with its `secret`), `event: { id, name }`, `allow_static` and `ticket_events` (the ticket site's events that are `ON_SALE` or `CLOSED` and start within 2 days or ended within 2 days); a pass from another event scans as `OTHER_EVENT` with `event_name` and no pass details ("Wrong event · This pass is for <event>, not this event."), in Entry and Pickup mode and in an offline queue synced later, and nothing is recorded. Only one phone can record the first entry; repeat scans are logged as `DUPLICATE`. Entry shows no name or age: staff just ask for ID. **Work picker:** at the top, "On D' Road" (Entry and Pickup) or "Tickets · <event>" for each ticket event on today (entry only; remembered on the phone as `door_work`). In ticket mode the phone keeps that event's tickets from `/api/door/tickets?event=<id>` (ref, kind, label, status, secret while valid, and the holder's and buyer's names only for Look up) and scans go through `createTicketDoor()` in tickets.js: checked in once (`DUPLICATE` after, with "Let them in anyway" = `OVERRIDE`), refunded or cancelled refused, a transferred ticket's old QR `TRANSFERRED` ("Re-issued"), another event's ticket `OTHER_EVENT`, a car pass shows a big "Car pass" ("Let one car in. A car pass does not let a person in."), and the label shows, never a name. A pass scanned in ticket mode, or a ticket in On D' Road mode, says "Switch mode" and nothing is looked up. **Look up** (the guest's phone is dead): Entry's search (and ticket mode's) finds name, email or reference, goes to Check ID ("Check their photo ID says <Name>"), and "Let them in" checks them in as `MANUAL` with the phone's name; that's the only time Entry shows a name. Ticket check-ins are in `ticket_checkins`; the admin's log is `/api/admin/ticket-checkins?event=<id>` (on `/admin/tickets`), offline scans sync the same way as passes, and a re-issued ticket reaches the phone at its next sync.
- **Package pickup:** the scanner's Pickup mode (or "Mark collected" on `/admin/pickup`) sets `orders.picked_up_at`/`picked_up_by`, once per paid order, and shows the guest's size. Repeat pickups are blocked and logged as `PICKUP_DUP`; undo is `PICKUP_UNDO`. Works offline like entry. Staff can search Pickup mode by name (door list passes carry `full_name`, null for cancelled orders); a search tap shows a Check ID screen with the name before handing over, and QR scans skip it. Hand over shows the name big next to "Check their photo ID says <Name>"; "Next guest" clears the search. From 900px wide (a laptop at the pickup desk) the search and list sit next to the camera; while a result is open the scanner behind it is `inert`, Escape goes Back (Check ID) or Next guest, and Tab stays on the result's buttons. `/admin/pickup` (the current event's packages and orders only) also has the size totals to order stock from, name search and a pickup list CSV (CSVs start with a UTF-8 byte-order mark so Excel shows accented names).
- **Pay-by deadline:** stored on each new order as `orders.pay_by`. `settings.pay_rule` is `days` (`pay_days` after reserving; 0 = no deadline) or `weekday` (the next `pay_weekday`, 0 = Sunday, at `pay_time`, Antigua time, at least 24 hours away). Older orders with no `pay_by` use their `pay_days` (see `dueSql` in payments.js, used by expiry, the reminder and PayPal's `syncDue`). The one reminder goes out 24 hours before the deadline when the guest had 48+ hours.
- **FAQ and sponsors:** `settings.faq` (`# ` starts a question; a missing key means `DEFAULT_FAQ`, an empty one hides the FAQ) and `settings.sponsors` (JSON `[{ name, logo, url }]`, up to 12; logos are https links or paths like `sponsors/acme.png` in the guest site repo). Both come back from `/api/site`.
- **Reset:** `/admin/reset` deletes either all guest data (guests and their `guest_event` bonuses, invites, login links, orders with their check-ins and pickups, group-email log, in every event, and the ticket site's orders, items, payments, tickets and ticket check-ins; keeps events (past ones stay, empty, with `rsvp_confirmed` cleared), packages, tiers, codes, promoters, the ticket events, products, tiers and menus, and settings, both locks included) or everything (also every event, package, tier, code, promoter and promoter allowance, every ticket event, product, tier, bottle and chaser, and every setting, back to the built-in defaults with one fresh current event "On D' Road" with 2 invites per guest, which means both sites are locked again; only `preview_version` and `tickets_preview_version` are kept, so a preview link that was reset never comes back). `POST /api/admin/reset/start { scope }` gives a one-time 6-digit number (10 minutes, one try, kept in memory); `POST /api/admin/reset { scope, code }` deletes in one transaction. The page shows paid orders and money received as a warning and can download a JSON copy first (every event's orders, invites, door scans and group emails, plus the events, the ticket site's data from `/api/admin/tickets/export`, and for "Everything" the promoters and setup too).
- **Ticket site (tickets.js, tickets.ondroad.xyz):** public, no invites and no roster: buyers never join `guests`. It sells the ticket events (`ticket_events`: name, `slug` for `?e=<slug>`, `starts_at`/`ends_at`, `venue`, `address`, `show_location` `AFTER` (default: shown once bought) or `PUBLIC`, `info`, `buyer_info` (for buyers only), `poster` (checked like the sponsor logos), `refund_policy`, an optional service fee (`fee_kind` `NONE`/`FLAT` per person/`PERCENT`), `status` `DRAFT`/`ON_SALE`/`CLOSED`/`PAST`, `sort_order`); several can be on sale at once, and they have nothing to do with the invite-only events in `events.js`. `/api/tickets/events` lists the on-sale ones (with `when`, `price_from`/`price_to` over the ticket products and the currency); `/api/tickets/events/:slug` gives one with its products (current price, `tier`, `next_tier`, `left`, `max_now`, `sold_out`, `sales_closed`, a table's slots, perks and deposit) and the menus; `/api/tickets/site` the contact, the ticket terms (`settings.tickets_terms`, falling back to `DEFAULT_TICKET_TERMS`; `# ` headings, `- ` bullets; deposits are non-refundable there) and the invite-only site's address.
- **Ticket lock:** a second `createLock()` with `tickets_lock`, `tickets_unlock_at`, `tickets_preview_version`, token prefix `'tickets-preview:'` and admin path `/api/admin/tickets-lock` (`/preview-reset`), the same rules as the main lock (a missing key means locked, Antigua time, 5-second cache). It covers `/api/tickets/*` only, except the signed order and ticket pages and their PayPal and transfer routes (bought tickets keep working while it's locked); the main lock never applies to `/api/tickets/*`. While locked `/api/tickets/site` answers only `{ locked: true }` (`no-store`); the preview link is `${TICKETS_URL}/?preview=<token>`, sent back as `x-preview`. The switch is the Ticket site lock section of `/admin/tickets`.
- **Products:** `ticket_products` (`kind` `TICKET` or `TABLE`, `name`, `includes` (the short line under the name), `description`, `price_cents`, `currency` `XCD`/`USD`, optional `capacity`, `max_per_order`, optional `sales_end`, `active`, `sort_order`), with price tiers in `ticket_tiers` (name, price, optional `ends_at` and `quantity`), the same rule as packages: the first tier that hasn't ended or sold out; none left means sales closed. A table also has `tickets_included`, `car_passes`, `bottle_slots` (a list of tiers 1-3, one per bottle), `slot_later` ("Decide later" allowed), `chaser_points`, `perks` (one per line), and an optional deposit (`deposit_kind` `NONE`/`PERCENT`/`AMOUNT`, `deposit_value`; `balance_rule` `DAYS` (`balance_days` after booking) or `DATE` (`balance_date`), never after the event starts). Prices show in EC$ with what the card is charged in US$ (`EC$100.00 · US$37.04`).
- **Menus (global):** `bottles` (`name`, `tier` 1-3 with the labels in `settings.bottle_tier_labels`, default Standard / Premium / Top shelf, `upgrade_cents`, `active`), `chaser_groups` (`name`, `points` each, e.g. Sodas 1, Juices 3) and `chasers`. A slot includes any bottle at or below its tier; a higher one costs that bottle's `upgrade_cents`. Chasers are picked with steppers within the table's points (kept in hundredths, so 1.5 + 1.5 is exactly 3); a remainder is fine, and "No chasers" is a choice.
- **Checkout:** `POST /api/tickets/checkout { event, items, buyer: { name, email, birth_date }, terms: true, total_cents }` (40 an hour per IP, 12 per email). The name goes through `cleanName`, the date of birth through `cleanBirthDate` (18+, Antigua's date). The backend prices everything itself (`priceOrder()`): a table is one item per build (`qty` 1, `bottles` per slot, `null` for decide later, `chasers [{ id, qty }]`). Codes: `TERMS_REQUIRED`, `NAME_INVALID`, `EMAIL_INVALID`, `UNDER_18`, `BIRTH_DATE_INVALID` (each with `field`), `EVENT_CLOSED`, `ITEMS_REQUIRED`, `PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE`, `SOLD_OUT`, `SALES_CLOSED`, `CURRENCY_MIX`, `BAD_QTY`, `MAX_PER_ORDER`, `BOTTLE_REQUIRED`, `BOTTLE_INVALID`, `CHASER_INVALID`, `CHASERS_OVER`, and `409 PRICE_CHANGED` when `total_cents` isn't what it costs now (with the fresh `event` and `total_cents`); `403 PAYPAL_CLOSED` ("Online payment isn't open yet. Check back soon.") when PayPal isn't on for this email (the same sandbox/`PAYPAL_TESTERS` rule as the main site). The product rows are locked (`FOR UPDATE`, in id order) while it counts what's held, so two buyers can't both take the last tickets. It creates a `PENDING` `ticket_orders` row (`ref` `TKT-1A2B3C4D`, buyer, `subtotal_cents`, `fee_cents`, `total_cents`, `deposit_cents` = due now, `currency`, `balance_due_at`, `terms_accepted_at`) with its `ticket_order_items` (unit price, tier, upgrades, line, deposit, tickets and car passes included, the `selections`), held for 30 minutes (`hold_until`), and answers `{ ref, s, hold_until, due_now_cents, total_cents, currency, paypal }`. What's held: paid and deposit-paid orders, and pending ones still inside their hold or with a payment PayPal is holding (`paypal_status` `PENDING` or `AMOUNT_MISMATCH`).
- **Ticket PayPal:** `/api/tickets/paypal/create-order|capture { ref, s }` (first payment: in full, or a table's deposit) and `/api/tickets/order/:ref/paypal/create-order|capture { s }` (the balance), all through paypal.js's plumbing: USD at 2.70 rounded up, invoice id = the ref (or `<ref>-BAL` for the balance), `paypal_purpose` `FULL`/`DEPOSIT`/`BALANCE`, an earlier payment settled before a new one is made (`ALREADY_PAID`, `PENDING`, `CHECK`), the capture checked against `paypal_amount`, `DECLINED` (`402`), and a hold that ran out is only paid if the tickets are still free (else `410 HOLD_EXPIRED`, cancelled, not charged). Each capture is a `ticket_payments` row (`kind`, `via` `PAYPAL`/`MANUAL`, `amount_cents`, `usd_amount`, `capture_id`, `refund_id`); `paid_cents` adds them up. Paid in full: `PAID`, the tickets are issued and emailed. A deposit: `DEPOSIT_PAID` and a "Deposit received" email with the balance and its date. No webhooks.
- **Deposits and balances:** tickets are always paid in full; only tables can have a deposit, and the capacity stays held while `DEPOSIT_PAID`. Tickets are issued only once the order is paid in full. The buyer pays the balance online from the order page ("Deposit paid: $X · Balance $Y due <date>" with Pay balance); the admin can mark it paid by hand (`/api/admin/tickets/orders/:id/mark-paid`, `via = 'MANUAL'`) or move the deadline (`/balance-due`, in the future and before the event starts). The job (`sweep()`, every 10 minutes with the payments job, or "Run checks now") recovers PayPal payments that didn't reach us, releases holds that ran out (not while PayPal holds a payment), sends one reminder 48 hours before a balance is due (when the buyer had more than that), and cancels bookings whose balance wasn't paid by the deadline ("Table released" email; the deposit is kept, `cancel_reason = 'BALANCE_UNPAID'`).
- **Tickets:** one `tickets` row per person (`kind = 'PERSON'`, `label` like "General admission" or "Premium cabana · 3 of 8") and per car pass (`kind = 'CAR'`, "Car pass · Premium cabana · 1 of 2"; scanned at the gate, separate from people), `ref` 12 characters of Crockford base32, `status` `VALID`/`TRANSFERRED`/`CANCELLED`, `version`, holder name and email. Links are signed: the order page `?order=<ref>&s=<HMAC('tkt-order:' + ref), 22 chars>` and each ticket `?ticket=<ref>&s=<HMAC('tkt-ticket:' + ref)>`; `/api/tickets/order/:ref?s=` and `/api/tickets/ticket/:ref?s=` (`no-store`, 404 `NOT_FOUND` on a wrong signature) return the order (status, money, items with selections, tickets with secrets while valid, the event with its location once bought and `buyer_info` once paid) or one ticket. **Transfer** (`POST /api/tickets/ticket/:ref/transfer { s, name, email }`, 20 an hour per IP): re-issues the ticket to the friend with a new ref and `version + 1` (`prev_id` keeps the history) and emails them their own link; the old ref, link, secret and QR stop working (`TRANSFERRED`, shown as "Transferred to f***@gmail.com"). Refused for a ticket that's been scanned (`USED`), cancelled, already transferred, an event that's over (`EVENT_OVER`), to the same email (`SAME_EMAIL`), or within a minute of its last move (`429 TOO_SOON`). **Find my tickets** (`POST /api/tickets/resend { email }`, 10 per 15 minutes per IP, 3 an hour per email) emails the links of every order bought with that email and every ticket transferred to it, always with the same answer.
- **Ticket refunds:** `/api/admin/tickets/orders/:id/refund` (paid or deposit-paid orders; the admin page asks first) refunds every PayPal capture of the order (a partly paid table: the deposit, and the balance if paid), stops at the first one PayPal refuses (nothing changes for it), then marks the order `REFUNDED`, cancels its tickets and emails the buyer. Manual payments are reported back for the organizers to repay.
- **Bar list and door list:** `/api/admin/tickets/events/:id/bar-list` (each table with its buyer, bottles (with "decide later") and chasers, plus bottle and chaser totals to order stock), its printable page `/admin/tickets/events/:id/bar-list` and `bar-list.csv`, and `door.csv` (one row per ticket); CSVs start with the UTF-8 BOM. Only paid and deposit-paid tables are on the bar list.
- **/admin/tickets ("Tickets & tables"):** in the overview's nav. Events (list; create, edit, put on sale, close, delete one with no orders; KPIs: sold, held, received per currency, balances to come, checked in; products with their tiers, "Visible/Hidden", edit, delete one with no orders; live sales: orders with search and a status filter, view, balance paid, extend, refund (asks first); the bar list on screen, Print and CSV; the door list CSV and the door log; "Run checks now"), Menus (bottles, chaser groups and chasers, the tier labels), Ticket site lock, and Terms (edit `tickets_terms`, or go back to the original draft). The Command Center shows a line "Ticket site: locked/open · N tickets sold today · N events on sale" (`/api/admin/tickets/summary`).
- Times shown to guests use the `America/Antigua` time zone.

## Database

`ensureSchema()` in `server.js` runs `ensureTicketSchema()` (tickets.js: `ticket_events`, `ticket_products`, `ticket_tiers`, `bottles`, `chaser_groups`, `chasers`, `ticket_orders`, `ticket_order_items`, `ticket_payments`, `tickets`, `ticket_checkins`, all `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`), then creates and migrates `events`, `guest_event` (`email`, `event_id`, `invite_bonus`), `promoters`, `promoter_event`, `packages`, `orders`, `settings`, `checkins`, `broadcasts`, `package_tiers` and `promo_codes` on every boot, adds `event_id` to `packages`, `orders`, `promo_codes`, `broadcasts` and `invites` (defaulting to `current_event_id()`) and `promoter_id` to `promo_codes` and `invites`, then runs `migrateEvents()` (events.js): the first time, with no events yet, it creates the current event "On D' Road" (2 invites per guest, the event details copied from settings) and turns each guest's legacy `invites_left` into a bonus; on every boot it fills any NULL `event_id` with the current event (an order with its package's event). It's safe to run again: a second boot creates and changes nothing. It also adds `guests.age_confirmed_at`, the sign-up columns `guests.full_name`, `guests.birth_date` (DATE), `guests.instagram`, `guests.approval` (`'PENDING'`, `'APPROVED'` or `'DECLINED'`; `NOT NULL DEFAULT 'APPROVED'`, so existing guests stay in), `guests.applied_at` and `guests.decided_at`, `packages.sizes`, `packages.pay_note`, `orders.size`, `orders.terms_accepted_at`, `orders.paid_via`, `orders.pay_days`, `orders.pay_by`, the order money columns (`tier_id`, `tier_name`, `list_cents`, `discount_cents`, `amount_cents`, `currency`, `promo_code`), `orders.picked_up_at`/`picked_up_by`, `orders.qr_version` (the live pass's version, 1; nothing bumps it yet) and the `orders.paypal_*` columns. Orders from before the money columns get the package price filled in once. Add new columns there with `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`. `guests`, `invites` and `login_links` are **not** created by code; they already exist in Aiven (columns are added to `invites`, as above).

## Conventions

- All SQL is parameterized. Multi-step writes use `pool.connect()` + `BEGIN`/`COMMIT`/`ROLLBACK`.
- Email helpers return an error or `null` and never throw. Guest emails are often sent after the response.
- Rate limiting is in memory: `rateLimit(max, windowMs, keyOf)`, per IP by default. Use `perUser` after `requireAuth` for logged-in actions, since guests on mobile data share IPs. `/api/login` is also limited per email address.
- `express.json({ limit: '10kb' })` applies to every route registered after it. Routes that need bigger bodies (door sync, terms) are registered before it with their own parser.
- Errors are `{ error: '<short plain message>' }`. Guest-facing text is plain, friendly and short.
- Admin and door pages are HTML strings inside the JS files, with no framework. They share the guest site's "Night Kinetic" look: matte charcoal and black (`#141416`, `#0a0a0b`) with safety orange (`--accent` `#ff5a1f`, ink text on it) as the one accent; `--good` acid green `#b6ff3b`, `--warn` amber, `--bad` red and cyan only for states; concrete greys (`#c9c8c3`/`#e4e3df`) for utility surfaces such as dialogs, toasts and previews. Each page's `:root` holds the palette. Archivo expanded for headings (the wordmark is a heavy "ON D'" with an orange apostrophe and a thin "ROAD"), Archivo for body, Space Mono for labels, bracket tags like `[ 18+ | ANTIGUA ]` and codes. Square corners, hairline rules, no shadows on buttons. Emails follow it with email-safe fonts (a heavy wide face such as Arial Black for headlines, a monospace for tags): a charcoal header with the wordmark, an orange button with dark text, "How to pay" on a concrete panel, and the QR black on white on a concrete ticket.
- CORS allows `FRONTEND_URL`, `https://ondroad.xyz`, `https://www.ondroad.xyz`, `https://tickets.ondroad.xyz`, `TICKETS_URL` and `EXTRA_ORIGINS`. `cors()` reflects the request's `Access-Control-Request-Headers`, so the guest site's `x-preview` header (site lock) passes the preflight; keep it that way if the CORS options change (or list `x-preview` in `allowedHeaders`).
- The frontend and backend deploy separately. When a backend change needs a frontend change, deploy the frontend first.

## Known issues (review of 2026-10-06)

1. No README, `.env.example` or tests in the repo.
2. The backend runs on Render's free plan: it sleeps when idle, so the first request is slow and the payments job doesn't run while asleep.

Fixed since the review: `/api/update-rsvp` only accepts `CONFIRMED` or `PENDING`; package edits refuse a bad price, capacity or name with a 400 instead of saving 0 or failing with a 500; removing a guest is refused while they have a paid order and cancels their unpaid reservation (orders of guests removed earlier are flagged "Guest was removed from the roster" in the Orders tab); each order keeps the `pay_days` it was reserved under (`orders.pay_days`; NULL on older orders means the current setting); reservation, invite, refund-request and PayPal limits count per account (`perUser`), not per IP.

One active order per guest per event is enforced by locking the guest's row in `/api/orders` (and holding the current event's row `FOR SHARE`), so two quick reservations can't both go through and none lands in an event that is ending.
